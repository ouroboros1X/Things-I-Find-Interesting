# 🧠 Knowledge

> **A collection of things I find interesting.**

This repository is my personal archive of ideas, research, concepts, technologies, experiments, and resources that I come across while exploring the world of technology and science.

It isn't a course, a roadmap, or a structured curriculum.

It's simply a place to keep the things that make me stop and think:

**"That's interesting. I want to understand this."**

---

## 📂 Structure

```text
knowledge/
│
├── papers/
│   ├── physics/
│   └── technology/
│
├── concepts/
├── technologies/
├── ideas/
├── experiments/
└── resources/
```

### 📄 Papers

Research papers that I come across and want to understand or revisit.

* `physics/` — Physics, quantum mechanics, astrophysics, cosmology, etc.
* `technology/` — AI, ML, LLMs, distributed systems, databases, computer systems, etc.

### 🧠 Concepts

Interesting concepts that are worth understanding, regardless of whether they belong to a specific project.

Examples:

* Distributed systems
* Compilers
* Operating systems
* Networking
* Cryptography
* AI

### ⚙️ Technologies

Technologies, tools, architectures, or systems that I find interesting and want to explore.

### 💡 Ideas

Random ideas, observations, questions, and things that might turn into something bigger later.

### 🔬 Experiments

Things I build or test simply to understand how something works.

### 🔗 Resources

Interesting articles, documentation, videos, websites, books, and other resources worth keeping.

---

## ✍️ What Goes Here?

Anything that makes me curious.

A research paper.

A weird computer architecture concept.

A new database design.

An obscure networking protocol.

A physics discovery.

A new AI technique.

A technology I've never seen before.

A random idea at 2 AM.

If I find it interesting enough to learn more about, it belongs here.

---

## 📝 Notes Format

For things that need deeper documentation, I'll generally try to capture:

```md
# Topic

**Source:**  
**Date:**  
**Category:**  

## What is it?

...

## Why is it interesting?

...

## What did I learn?

...

## My thoughts

...

## References

- ...
```

The format isn't strict.

The goal is **understanding, not documentation for documentation's sake.**

---

## 🌌 Philosophy

> **Don't try to learn everything.**
>
> **Collect the things that make you curious, and understand them deeply.**

This repository is a snapshot of my curiosity over time.

It will probably become messy.

That's fine.

The purpose isn't to make a perfect knowledge base.

The purpose is to **keep learning.**
